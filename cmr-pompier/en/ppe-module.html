Contamination tracking for firefighters' fire kitPPE module

Track the contamination of your personal protective equipment (PPE, EPI in French) after every call-out.

Simple tracking of the use, contamination, cleaning and return to service of fire kit (jacket, trousers, fire hood, F1 helmet and fire boots), linked to the call-out reports in Carnet Expo CMR (CMR exposure record).

  • Reduce the risksLimit secondary exposure to CMR (carcinogenic, mutagenic, reprotoxic) agents.
  • End-to-end trackingUse, contamination, cleaning, replacement.
  • Proof of traceabilityFor medical follow-up and prevention.
