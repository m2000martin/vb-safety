Carnet Expo CMR · contamination reporting software for French fire and rescue services (SDIS)Your CMR exposures on call-outs, tracked and recorded.

At the end of every call-out, the crew commander (CA) or incident commander (COS) writes the contamination report for their crew. Command checks that everything is up to date, the occupational health and medical service (SSSM) reviews and completes it, and every firefighter can access their own history.

Why?

A real and recognised exposure

Firefighting as an occupation has been classified as carcinogenic to humans (Group 1) by IARC (International Agency for Research on Cancer) since 2022. Whatever is not recorded on the day of the call-out is lost, both for medical follow-up and for any future recognition as an occupational disease.

Ready for your obligations

What the regulations require, the Carnet produces

The reports completed after each call-out feed directly into the documents the texts require. The SSSM doctor checks, signs off, then downloads.

DGSCGC circular (circulaire DGSCGC) · 14 January 2025

Annual record and exposure certificate

An annual record of potentially exposing activities for each firefighter, before the medical check-up, and a cumulative certificate to be kept for 50 years. Separate templates for career and volunteer firefighters.

Generated and signed off by the doctor in the SSSM area
Decree no. 2025-1349 (décret n° 2025-1349) · 26 December 2025

Training, overhaul and cleaning count too

Occupational disease tables no. 16 bis and 30 (tableaux n° 16 bis et 30) cover firefighting, including exposing training, overhaul and equipment cleaning.

Smoke-house sessions, live-fire training and equipment cleaning, declared in one form
French Labour Code (Code du travail) · R. 4412-93-1

List of exposed workers and individual record

Nature, duration and degree of each exposure to CMR agents, sent to the health service and kept for at least 40 years.

Roles

Five roles, each limited to what concerns them

The CA or COS writes the report, command checks that everything is up to date, the SSSM reviews and completes it, and firefighters view their own history. The personal protective equipment (PPE, EPI in French) module is separate and reserved for the station's PPE officer.

Firefighter

Firefighter deployed on the call-out
  • Views their exposures, call-out by call-out
  • Follows their history and individual exposure record
  • Reports a change of fire kit or requests one
  • Exports their data as PDF or CSV
  • Has nothing to enter: their CA or COS takes care of it
See this view in the demo

Cannot see other firefighters' exposure

CA / COS

Crew commander or incident commander
  • Writes the contamination and decontamination report at the end of the call-out
  • Does so for themselves and every member of their crew
  • Checks the positions, suggested from each person's post
  • Declares the crew's decontamination with a single tap, without entering the time
See this view in the demo

Sees only the call-outs they commanded

Station officer / command

Station commander, group commander, command
  • Sees whether every call-out has its report
  • Spots reports that are up to date, pending or overdue
  • Sends a reminder to the CA or COS concerned
  • Follows large-scale call-outs relief shift by relief shift
  • Exports the tracking status as CSV
See this view in the demo

Sees no individual exposure data

SSSM

Doctors and nurses of the medical service
  • Views all contamination reports
  • Corrects or completes each report
  • Follows each firefighter's full history
  • Produces the exposure record by year, over 12 months or over the whole career
  • Signs off the annual record and the exposure certificate of each firefighter
See this view in the demo

Every view and correction is logged

PPE officer

The station's fire kits
  • Sees which firefighters need re-equipping after each report
  • Records fire kit changes and items sent for laundering
  • Tracks the fires and washes of each fire kit, with an alert threshold
  • Edits firefighters' fire kit numbers
