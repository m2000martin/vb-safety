Health data

Security and health data

A firefighter's exposure data is health data, to be kept for decades. Here is how Carnet Expo CMR protects it, and what changes between the demo and your fire service's version.

French Public Health Code (Code de la santé publique) · L. 1111-8

Certified health-data hosting (HDS)

In production, data is hosted in France by a host certified for health data, or on your fire service's own infrastructure if it allows. The current demo contains no real data.

Medical confidentiality

Everyone sees only what concerns them

Individual exposure is restricted to the firefighter and the SSSM. Command sees the status of reports, never their content. The administrator can restrict access, never widen it. The rules are enforced by the server.

Traceability

Access and corrections logged

Every view and every correction of a report is recorded. Once approved by the doctor, the report is locked. Annual records carry the doctor's sign-off and its date.

40 to 50 years

Retention over a whole career

At least 40 years for data on exposure to CMR agents (French Labour Code (Code du travail), R. 4412-93-3), 50 years for the exposure certificate (DGSCGC circular of 14 January 2025, circulaire DGSCGC du 14 janvier 2025). Export formats remain open: CSV and PDF.

GDPR (RGPD)

Impact assessment prepared with your DPO

We provide the description of the processing, the register and the data protection impact assessment (DPIA, AIPD in French) template. No trackers, no advertising services. Firefighters are informed of their rights.

Reversibility

Your data remains yours

The data belongs to the fire service. It can be exported at any time, in full, in open formats. The contract provides for its return and deletion at the end of the service.

Assistant

No exposure data sent to the AI

The assistant opens, searches and explains. It receives only the user's sentence, never their exposure data. It fills in and approves nothing, and the administrator can switch it off.

Interoperability

National formats and open exports

Call-outs received in the national exchange format (CISU, used by NexSIS), export of fire kit movements for your clothing-store software, CSV exports for the SSSM.

From demo to service

Demo and fire service version

The demo is for seeing the tool. Your fire service's version is installed on suitable hosting, with your accounts and your settings.
