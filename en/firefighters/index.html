Firefighter exposure trackingEvery fire leaves a trace. We make it visible.

A firefighter exposure record logs, call by call, what each firefighter was exposed to: type of fire, position held, duration, protection and decontamination. VB Safety helps French fire services (SDIS) keep it for every firefighter and send it to their medical service (SSSM), with the Carnet Expo CMR, a contamination reporting tool.

What is at stake

A recognised risk, exposures still poorly recorded

01

Exposures that go unrecorded

In 2024 the French Senate noted that exposure forms were not filled in systematically. In 2023, the public-sector pension fund (CNRACL) recorded only 24 occupational disease claims from firefighters.

02

Decontamination that is hard to prove

Undressing zone, bagged gear, wipes, shower: the steps exist, but nothing shows they were actually done, firefighter by firefighter.

03

Effects that appear decades later

Without a dated, individual record, linking a disease to past calls is very hard when seeking recognition.

How it works

4 steps after each call

  1. The crew commander reports

    For the whole crew: role held, breathing apparatus, degree of contamination, decontamination steps.

  2. Command checks

    Does every call have its report? The station chief sees what is missing and sends reminders, without access to individual data.

  3. The medical service reviews

    It views and corrects if needed, adds measurements and samples, then validates and locks the report.

  4. The firefighter keeps the proof

    Every dated exposure, the cumulative history, exportable at any time.

Regulation (France)

What the texts say

  1. 2022IARC · Monograph 132

    Firefighting classified as carcinogenic to humans

    Sufficient evidence for mesothelioma and bladder cancer; limited evidence for five other cancers.

    Read the IARC monograph
  2. 2024Decree no. 2024-307
    In force

    A list of workers exposed to CMR agents

    French employers keep an up-to-date list of exposed workers; the information is kept for at least forty years.

    Read the decree on Légifrance (FR)
  3. 2025Bill
    Passed by the Senate

    An exposure record for every firefighter

    Each fire service would keep an exposure record for every career or volunteer firefighter exposed to CMR agents, following a national template.

    Read the text passed by the Senate (FR)
  4. 2025Decree no. 2025-1349
    In force

    Occupational diseases: tables 16 bis and 30

    Firefighting work is now listed in the occupational disease tables for combustion soot and asbestos.

    Read the decree on Légifrance (FR)

Frequently asked questions

What fire services ask us

Who enters the information after a call?

The crew commander or the incident commander, for themselves and their crew. They know the roles held, the use of breathing apparatus and the decontamination conditions. Firefighters have nothing to enter.

Where is the data hosted?

The demo runs on a server in France, with fictitious data. For a real deployment, exposure data is health data: it is hosted by a certified health data host (HDS) or on the fire service's own infrastructure.

Are volunteer firefighters covered?

Yes. They breathe the same smoke as career firefighters, and the bill passed by the French Senate in 2025 explicitly includes them.

Can the tool be adapted to our fire service?

Yes: call types, decontamination protocol, validation circuit, roles and export formats for the medical service can all be configured.

Is the tool available in English?

The tool is currently in French, for French fire services. Contact us if you work outside France.
