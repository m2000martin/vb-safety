CMR exposure tracking on construction sitesCMR tracking is built site after site.

Since July 2024, every French construction company must keep a list of its workers exposed to CMR agents such as crystalline silica or wood dust. With VB Safety, one sentence from the team leader is enough: the tool recognises the workers, the task, the duration and the protection, then keeps the list and individual records up to date.

What is at stake

CMR agents every day, tracking once a year

01

Everyday exposures

Cutting and drilling concrete or stone (silica), woodwork, diesel machinery, old asphalt and tar (PAHs): CMR agents are part of routine tasks.

02

An obligation that relies on memory

The exposed workers list and records are often filled in after the fact, from memory, when the risk assessment is updated.

03

Documents expected by occupational health

Nature, duration and degree of exposure must be sent to the occupational health service and kept for decades.

How it works

From the team leader's sentence to the risk assessment

  1. Record from the site

    The team leader describes the task in one sentence, on their phone.

    “Karim and Lucas, dry-cutting blocks 2 h, FFP3”

  2. Track per worker

    The tool identifies the CMR agents involved and gives each worker an indicative exposure level.

  3. Document

    The exposed workers list and individual records update automatically.

  4. Update the risk assessment

    The chemical risk assessment is pre-filled; the employer validates it and adds it to the single risk assessment document.

Regulation (France)

The employer's obligations (France)

  1. Labour codeArt. R. 4412-59 and following
    In force

    CMR risk prevention

    Assess exposures, substitute where possible, reduce to the lowest technically possible level, inform and train workers.

  2. 2021Order of 26 October 2020
    In force

    Crystalline silica classified as carcinogenic

    Work exposing to respirable crystalline silica dust from work processes has been on the list of carcinogenic processes since 1 January 2021.

    Read the order on Légifrance (FR)
  3. 2022Decree no. 2022-395
    In force

    Risk assessment kept for 40 years

    Successive versions of the single risk assessment document are kept for at least forty years.

    Read the decree on Légifrance (FR)
  4. 2024Decree no. 2024-307
    In force

    A list of workers exposed to CMR agents

    Up-to-date list of workers likely to be exposed, with the nature, duration and degree of exposure when known; kept for at least forty years by the occupational health service.

    Read the decree on Légifrance (FR)
Frequently asked questions

What companies ask us

Do we need to install an app?

No. It runs in the browser, on phone and computer.

Does the tool store medical data?

No. It records tasks, durations, agents and protections. Medical follow-up stays with the occupational health service.

Which CMR agents are covered?

The first version covers common construction agents: crystalline silica, wood dust, diesel engine emissions and PAHs. Each version adds more.

What about asbestos?

Asbestos has its own regulatory regime (surveys, operating procedures, work monitoring). It is not covered by the first version.

Is the tool available in English?

The tools are currently in French, built on French regulation. Contact us if you work outside France.
