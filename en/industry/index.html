What is at stake

Diffuse exposures, stricter obligations

01

Many sources of exposure

Maintenance, cleaning, machining, welding, woodwork, diesel vehicles and machinery: CMR exposures are spread across many jobs and subcontractors.

02

More precise requirements

Since July 2024, the exposed workers list must state, when known, the nature, duration and degree of each worker's exposure.

03

Data that is rarely usable

Measurements, job sheets and schedules are scattered: hard to say who was exposed, to what and for how long.

How it works

A step-by-step rollout, site by site

  1. Map

    Jobs, tasks and CMR agents involved, based on your existing risk assessment.

  2. Record

    Team leaders or HSE staff describe the tasks carried out; each worker involved is linked.

  3. Measure

    On pilot sites, contamination measurement backs up the risk situations.

  4. Document

    Exposed workers list, individual records and risk assessment basis update themselves.

Regulation (France)

The employer's obligations (France)

  1. Labour codeArt. R. 4412-59 and following
    In force

    CMR risk prevention

    Assess exposures, substitute where possible, reduce to the lowest technically possible level, inform and train workers.

  2. 2022Decree no. 2022-395
    In force

    Risk assessment kept for 40 years

    Successive versions of the single risk assessment document are kept for at least forty years.

    Read the decree on Légifrance (FR)
  3. 2024Decree no. 2024-307
    In force

    Exposed workers list and limit values

    Up-to-date list of workers exposed to carcinogenic, mutagenic and reprotoxic agents, kept for at least forty years; stricter limit values for benzene, acrylonitrile and nickel compounds.

    Read the decree on Légifrance (FR)
Frequently asked questions

What HSE teams ask us

Which industries are concerned?

Any where workers are exposed to CMR agents: metalworking, chemicals, maintenance, woodwork, logistics with diesel machinery, surface treatment, among others.

Does it connect to our existing tools?

Imports (staff list, jobs, schedules) are defined with each pilot site, to avoid entering data twice.

Do you store medical data?

No. The tool records tasks, durations, agents and protections. Medical follow-up stays with the occupational health service.

What technology does on-site measurement use?

It is a quick on-site measurement, currently being validated. Details are shared with pilot sites under a confidentiality agreement.

How do we start?

Describe your site and exposed jobs through the contact form: we will propose a suitable pilot.
